Biodynamic Craniosacral Therapy (BCST) is a complementary, light-touch practice grounded in presence and careful listening.

What is the approach?

Sessions involve gentle contact and attention to your experience of the body. The biodynamic approach emphasizes stillness, awareness and the body’s inherent resources.

Meeta brings a quiet, attentive presence and her background in development and special education to this work.

What might a session involve?

A conversation about your needs and comfort, followed by quiet, light-touch work. Craniosacral sessions are generally received fully clothed. Discuss the position, contact and any adaptations with Meeta beforehand.

Is this a massage?

The touch is usually much lighter than massage and does not involve muscle kneading. Ask about the approach before deciding whether it is right for you.

Can I pause or ask for a change?

Yes. Tell the practitioner about discomfort, ask questions and request a pause or stop at any time. Your preferences and consent matter throughout.

How many sessions will I need?

There is no fixed answer for everyone. Discuss your aims, suitability and any proposed schedule directly with Meeta; outcomes cannot be guaranteed.

Does it replace medical treatment?

No. Craniosacral therapy is complementary. Evidence is limited for many proposed uses, and it should not replace diagnosis or medical or mental-health treatment. Discuss relevant health concerns before beginning.
