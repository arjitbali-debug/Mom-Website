Samarpan and SOPAN

Leadership in autism education

SOPAN

SOPAN is the Society of Parents of Children with Autistic Disorders, a registered NGO working in autism and related developmental disabilities. It began in 2002 as an initiative of parents seeking education and training for their children.

Samarpan

Samarpan was established by SOPAN in February 2003 as a centre for children with autism. Dr. Bali served as its Principal.

Research-informed practice

Communication through visual strategies

In a 2007 study co-authored with Dr. Rubina Lal, 30 children with autism participated in research on visual strategies using objects, pictures, symbols and manual signs. The study reported significant improvement in communication skills for the intervention group.

HANDLE & autism

Understanding the person
behind the learning.

HANDLE stands for Holistic Approach to NeuroDevelopment and Learning Efficiency. It considers how a person’s sensory, movement and learning systems interact with their environment.

Rather than a standard set of exercises for everyone, HANDLE describes an individualized approach: observing how a person functions and offering gentle activities with attention to comfort, effort and signs of stress. The aim is to support participation and learning at an appropriate pace.

Meeta’s doctoral research

HANDLE in her Ph.D.

SNDT Women’s University’s research catalogue records Meeta’s 2012 Ph.D. thesis: Effectiveness of HANDLE and SI intervention techniques on Perceptual cognitive and behavioral skills of children with ASD.

Her research examined HANDLE and sensory integration interventions in the context of autism. This background informs her interest in individualized developmental support; the catalogue entry does not provide the study’s findings.
