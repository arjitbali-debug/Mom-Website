Common questions

Before you get in touch.

I’m new to all of this. Where should I start?

Begin with Ways to work together for an overview. If you are unsure, send a brief enquiry; you do not need to select a therapy before reaching out.

What is craniosacral therapy?

A complementary light-touch practice. The biodynamic approach emphasizes stillness and body awareness. Read the introduction and session FAQs to learn more.

What does “somatic” mean?

Somatic means relating to the body as it is experienced from within—for example, noticing sensations and how you feel. In Meeta’s profile, body awareness sits alongside developmental and systemic understanding.

What is family constellation work?

An experiential exploration of relational patterns and roles. It can offer material for reflection; it does not establish facts about other people. Explore constellation work.

Does Meeta work with children and families?

Her background is in special education with a focus on autism, communication and developmental support. Read about educational support and enquire about your child’s needs.

What are the fees and how long is a session?

Ask Meeta for the current fee, duration and cancellation terms for the type of session you are considering. Confirm these details before booking.

Are online appointments available?

Yes. Meeta welcomes clients internationally and offers online sessions by video call, including Zoom, depending on the modality. In-person sessions are available at her Mumbai clinic. Discuss suitability and time zones when booking.

Do I need to provide medical details in my first message?

No. A brief description of your enquiry is enough. Avoid sending medical reports or sensitive information in your initial email.

Are these approaches a substitute for medical or mental-health care?

No. Complementary practices should sit alongside appropriate care. Discuss suitability with your healthcare professional when relevant; results are individual and cannot be promised.
