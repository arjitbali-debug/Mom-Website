Common questions

How do I book?

Message +91 91679 08887. Meeta responds within 24 hours and arranges appointments directly. Please do not call.

Do I need to choose a modality first?

No. Briefly explain what you would like to discuss, and ask Meeta which part of her practice is relevant.

Can I consult from outside Mumbai or India?

Yes. Online sessions are available by video call, including Zoom, depending on the modality. Share your location and time zone when arranging an appointment.

What if my preferred language is different?

Clients from all language backgrounds are welcome. Let Meeta know your preferred language so that communication arrangements can be discussed.

What are the fees and session duration?

Ask Meeta for the fee, duration and cancellation arrangements for the session you are considering.

Can I ask about becoming a student?

Yes. Visit the teaching page and message Meeta about your interest.

What does “somatic” mean?

In this context, it means paying attention to your experience of the body, such as sensations and comfort. Meeta’s approach includes this attention to body awareness.
