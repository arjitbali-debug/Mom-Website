Breathwork and Rebirthing are part of Meeta’s wider practice, alongside body awareness and developmental approaches.

A personal exploration.

Breathwork involves bringing attention to breathing and your experience of it. Different methods can vary considerably in pace and intensity. Rebirthing is a particular approach within this broader field.

Meeta’s professional profile lists Rebirthing and Breathwork training with the Sadgamaya Foundation. Her work is guided by an interest in presence, embodiment and connection.

Ask about the specific technique, how a session is paced, and whether it is suitable for your circumstances.

Before you begin

Share relevant health concerns directly with the practitioner before a session. Some breathing practices may be unsuitable for certain medical or mental-health conditions.

You can ask questions, decline an exercise or stop. The approach should be discussed with you in advance.

Rebirthing & Breathwork
with D. P. Mahesh.

Meeta’s Rebirthing & Breathwork training is with the Sadgamaya Foundation. Its founder, D. P. Mahesh, is based in Bengaluru and teaches 5 Elements Breathwork.

Mahesh’s biography describes his training with Leonard Orr, the founder of Rebirthing Breathwork. His resources offer an introduction to the tradition in which Meeta studied.
