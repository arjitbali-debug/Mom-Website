Privacy

How contact links and external resources work on this website.

Contacting Meeta

This website has no consultation form or booking database. Text, WhatsApp and email links open the corresponding service. Nothing is sent until you send the message there. Those services have their own privacy practices.

A brief enquiry is enough to begin. You can ask Meeta directly how consultation correspondence and records are handled.

Videos & external links

Embedded videos load from YouTube only after you select Play. Once loaded, YouTube may process technical information under its privacy policy. You can also use the direct YouTube link. Reading links lead to external websites.

Website hosting

The site includes no analytics, advertising scripts or external fonts. The hosting provider may process routine technical information needed to deliver the website.
