HANDLE & autism

Understanding the person
behind the learning.

HANDLE stands for Holistic Approach to NeuroDevelopment and Learning Efficiency. It considers how a person’s sensory, movement and learning systems interact with their environment.

Rather than a standard set of exercises for everyone, HANDLE describes an individualized approach: observing how a person functions and offering gentle activities with attention to comfort, effort and signs of stress. The aim is to support participation and learning at an appropriate pace.

HANDLE in her Ph.D.

SNDT Women’s University’s research catalogue records Meeta’s 2012 Ph.D. thesis: Effectiveness of HANDLE and SI intervention techniques on Perceptual cognitive and behavioral skills of children with ASD.
