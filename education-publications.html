Published research · 2007

Visual strategies & communication

Effect of Visual Strategies on Development of Communication Skills in Children with Autism
Rubina Lal and Meeta Bali
Asia Pacific Disability Rehabilitation Journal, 18(2).[2]

The study involved 30 children. Communication scores improved significantly within the visual-strategies group. The difference in post-intervention scores between that group and the comparison group was not statistically significant.
