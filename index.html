Dr. Meeta Bali · Mumbai

A space for stillness and balance.

Dr. Meeta Bali is a special educator with a focus on autism, a Registered Craniosacral Therapist (RCST®), a constellation facilitator and a Rebirthing & Breathwork practitioner.

Her career began in special education, and she later served as principal of Samarpan, Centre for Autism Spectrum Disorders.[1] She holds a Ph.D. from SNDT Women’s University, where she researched HANDLE and sensory integration interventions for children with autism.[2]

Meeta brings a calm, attentive presence to her work. Her approach makes room for listening, personal experience and the search for greater ease and balance.

About Meeta

From special education to a wider practice

Working with children and families shaped Meeta’s interest in communication, development and the different ways people experience the world. Her later studies brought bodywork, breathwork and constellation work into her practice.

She values patience, compassion and deep listening. Her intention is to offer a calm space where people can feel heard and explore what balance means in their own lives.
