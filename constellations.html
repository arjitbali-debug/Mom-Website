Business & organizations

A systemic lens on organizations, leadership, teams, succession and family enterprises. Enquire about the question or context you would like to explore.

Trauma & shadow constellations

Pacing & sensitivity

Explore difficult experiences and aspects of ourselves we may reject, hide or struggle to acknowledge. The work invites reflection with attention to boundaries, pacing and overwhelm. Discuss suitability and existing support before participating.

Illness & disease constellations

Making space for lived experience

A reflective exploration of the emotional and relational experience of living with illness, including its effects on identity, family and belonging. This work does not diagnose disease, identify its cause or promise a cure, and is offered alongside appropriate healthcare.

What happens in constellation work?

A concern or question is explored through the relationships between elements of a system. Depending on the format, people or markers may represent those elements. The process invites reflection and perspective, rather than establishing facts about other people or prescribing one interpretation.

Do I need to know my whole family history?

You can start by asking about the concern you have. Discuss with Meeta what background would be helpful for the proposed session.

Is this individual work or a group workshop?

Constellation work can take different formats. Contact Meeta to ask what she currently offers and whether it fits your needs.

Is constellation work psychotherapy?

It is a distinct experiential practice and is not a replacement for psychotherapy, psychiatric care or specialist trauma treatment. Its interpretations should not be treated as diagnoses or evidence of events.
