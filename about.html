About Meeta

A whole-person approach.
A personal journey.

Special educator, craniosacral practitioner, constellation facilitator, breathwork practitioner, researcher and author.

In my own words

Meeting the whole person.

My journey has always been connected with understanding the human being: the mind, body, emotions, relationships, and the deeper patterns that shape our lives.

I am a Registered Craniosacral Therapist (RCST®) and Biodynamic Craniosacral Practitioner, a Family Constellation Therapist, Breathwork Practitioner, and Special Educator with a focus on Autism Spectrum Disorder.

My professional journey began in special education, working closely with children with autism and their families. Each individual has a unique way of experiencing and communicating with the world. That experience deepened my understanding of the importance of looking beyond symptoms and behaviours to connect with the whole person.

From education to an integrative practice

Over the years, my work expanded into approaches concerned with the nervous system, body, emotions, relationships and the deeper layers of human experience. I trained in Biodynamic Craniosacral Therapy, Family Constellation work, Breathwork and Rebirthing, and developmental and somatic approaches.

My background informs the way I think about development, trauma, embodiment and human potential. Somatic work means paying attention to our lived experience of the body; nervous-system-oriented approaches bring attention to regulation and connection.

Presence, compassion and deep listening

In my craniosacral practice, I offer a quiet and deeply listening presence. The work is gentle and non-invasive, guided by the biodynamic philosophy of supporting the body’s inherent resources for regulation, balance and healing.

Through Family Constellation work, I explore relational and systemic patterns that may influence our lives, including those we may not yet be aware of. This offers a way to reflect on relationships, family patterns and personal challenges, and consider a new perspective.

My approach is integrative. To me, healing is not about fixing a person. It is about creating conditions in which greater awareness, connection, regulation and wholeness can emerge. I believe that every person carries an inherent wisdom and capacity for healing. My role is to accompany each individual with presence, respect, compassion and deep listening.

“Healing begins when we are able to meet ourselves—and our experiences—with presence, compassion, and deep listening.”

Professional journey

Experience that informs the work.

Special education
More than three decades in special education, beginning at the B.M. Institute of Mental Health in Ahmedabad, followed by work in Mumbai, Gurgaon and Kanpur.
School leadership
Former Principal of Samarpan, Centre for Autism Spectrum Disorders, a project of SOPAN—the Society of Parents of Children with Autistic Disorders.
Research & writing
Work on visual strategies for communication and HANDLE-based interventions for children with autism, alongside a contribution listed in Autism: Pathways to an Inclusive Life.
Learning & teaching
Craniosacral training with ICSB, Switzerland, and experience as a training assistant with Quanta Health Care Solutions. Meeta is also an International Teacher in Training under BCTA/NA.
